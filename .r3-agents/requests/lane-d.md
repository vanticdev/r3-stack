# Lane D (from-scratch readiness): contract changes and touch points in shared files

All changes are additive. Merge notes for the coordinator:

## Spec and catalog contracts
- `r3_model/spec.py`:
  - `HardwareBinding.interface` gains `csi`.
  - `Environment.visual` (dict): the camera scene.
  - New `Fabrication` and `LoadCase` models, added as `RobotSpec.fabrication`.
- `r3_catalog/schema.py`:
  - `ComponentType.kind` gains `camera` and `cable`.
  - New `Material` and `Printer` models, with `Catalog.materials`, `Catalog.printers` and
    `Catalog.material()`.
  - `Catalog.print_fit()` now matches whole words, so "plastic" no longer matches PLA. The rc-car
    materials resolve exactly as before.
- `r3_catalog/__init__.py`: the loader reads `materials.yaml`, `printers.yaml` and `connectors_*.yaml`,
  and resolves `extends: <id>` component variants (a deep merge).
- `data/components/rc_car.yaml`: the `raspberry-pi-5-8gb` entry gains the CAM0/CAM1 22-pin CSI connectors
  and their pins. These are additive lines in `pins:` and `connectors:`. If lane A also edited this
  entry, keep both.
- `data/connector_geometry.yaml`: regenerated by `python -m r3_connectors.derive --write`, with new
  `KICAD`/`PROXIES` specs in `derive.py` and an `ffc_end` builder in `proxy.py`. On a merge conflict,
  merge `derive.py` and re-run the generator.
- `data/fasteners.yaml`: appended `nut-m4-nyloc`.

## Simulation (lane C area): two small hooks
- `r3_sim/kernel.py`:
  - `self.cameras = {}`;
  - `camera.sample(t1, state)` in `_post`, step 7.
- `r3_sim/hal_server.py`: `camera.*` ops (`_camera`).
- `r3_sim/vehicle/mjcf.py`: `add_scene(...)` before the bodies, and `add_cameras(...)` beside the chase
  camera. Both are no-ops for the rc-car: it has no `visual` and no camera component.
- `r3_sim/electrical/models/__init__.py`: registers `camera.csi` as a constant load.

## Orchestrator
- `stages.py`:
  - new stage `fabrication` (`r3_fab.stage`) after `cad`;
  - readiness precheck (`readiness.py`) that returns `not_configured` / `not_applicable`;
  - `status_of` gains those two statuses (the web types are updated too).
- `release.py`: a `not_configured` stage counts as `not_run`.
- `model_stage.py`:
  - spare, unbound computer inputs give info `computer_inputs_unused`;
  - custom parts are unions of their catalog solids, not bounding boxes;
  - interference is skipped when a current exact `assembly.json` exists
    (`interference_checked_by_assembly`);
  - new check `cable_too_short` / `cable_slack_low`.
- `assembly_stage.py`: `assembly.json` records `interference_method` and `inputs`.
- `sourcing.py` and `r3_catalog/sourcing/providers/catalog.py`: a `catalog` provider. Choosing one of
  its candidates pins the existing entry.
- `mcp_server.py`: adds `r3_get_catalog_component`, `r3_put_catalog_component` and `r3_guide`.
  `r3_create_project` defaults to `blank`.

## Known gap for lane B (failsafe without a custom PCB)
`pi_hang` expects `arbiter_neutral_within_s`, which needs the HAT arbiter. A car built only from
off-the-shelf parts has no arbiter. The twin models neither a Pi hardware watchdog nor an off-the-shelf
PWM failsafe module, so the acceptance agent cannot pass `pi_hang` without a custom board. Consider
modelling a bought failsafe, for example a servo-signal watchdog module, as a catalog part with
`failsafe_arbiter` behaviour.
