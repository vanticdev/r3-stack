# R3 — Engineering bench

## Point of view
A robot is a physical claim that needs evidence. R3 should feel like a carefully typeset engineering notebook joined to a CAD workstation: direct, exact and quiet. The primary visual hierarchy is **object → measurement → limit → provenance → next action**. The interface spends space on work, not on reassuring decoration. These designs were derived from functions and API contracts without inspecting the current UI; visual difference from that unseen UI cannot be empirically certified.

## Typography
Use IBM Plex Sans for navigation and prose; IBM Plex Mono for identifiers, code, measurements and tabular numerals. Desktop reference canvas 1536 × 1024. Page title 30–32 px/38, section title 18/24, body 15/22, table 14/20, metadata 12/18. Avoid all-caps paragraphs; short uppercase table group labels are acceptable. Numbers align at their decimal or right edge; units are in column headers or repeated beside standalone values.

## Color and material
Warm paper #F4F3EF for the work surface; white #FFFFFF for tables and inspectors; ink #202B30; secondary text #59666A; hairline #D3D8D6. Deep petroleum #183C45 anchors the narrow navigation and selected controls. Muted teal #27666A identifies selection and links. Pass uses dark green #336349, fail uses rust #B34232, stale/estimated uses ochre #906719; unknown uses gray plus the word Unknown. Always pair color with a word, shape, or explicit comparison. No gradients, glow, or ornamental shadows. Geometry uses neutral materials; highlight only the selected object or offending area.

## Layout and spacing
A 176 px vertical navigation rail, a 56 px context bar, then a rectangular working surface. Page padding 28 px; 4 px base unit; table rows roughly 40 px. Thin rules organize contiguous regions, rather than floating rounded cards. Buttons are rectangular, 2 px corner radius at most. One prominent action per page; other actions are quiet text or outlined controls. Project pages share all eleven section links and an app-level return. App pages share Projects, Model bank and Environment links.

## Data and state conventions
Use plain status words, short vertical marks and evidence rows instead of pill badges. Display measured value and limit side by side, including the inequality. Never show an invented readiness percentage. Current, stale, estimated, not run, not configured and not applicable are distinct. Empty values use an em dash with explanation, not 0. Distinguish priced subtotal from complete purchasing cost. Timestamps and input revisions establish provenance. Charts require labelled axes, units and check thresholds; no decorative sparklines. Tables may be dense; prioritize a clear selected row and detailed inspector over microtype.

## 3D and domain surfaces
CAD, wiring and board surfaces are large embedded work areas with neutral backgrounds, restrained axes/scale, an object tree or list and a numerical inspector. The model is grounded with subtle natural shading, without dramatic lighting or a beauty-shot backdrop. Wiring overlays carry physical routes, endpoint labels and a clearance callout. Simulation places playback and labelled time-series on a shared time cursor. Overview and diagnostics are evidence documents, not thumbnail galleries.

## Coherent illustrative fixture
RC-01 is a 1/10-scale autonomous RC car, spec v0.4.2. Owned: Raspberry Pi 5 4 GB, MPU-6050 breakout, Arducam UC-717. Other parts: RTK GPS breakout (exact model pending), ESC, 2S 5000 mAh LiPo, steering servo, 5 V buck and carrier HAT. Example mass 2.34 kg against 2.50 kg limit. A steering harness has 184 mm routed length, 212 mm cut length including 28 mm allowance; 15% nominal slack rounds to this allowance. Its worst dynamic clearance is 2.1 mm against ≥5.0 mm at 1.20 s; static clearance is 7.6 mm. Other example checks include 5 V rail at 4.92 V against ≥4.75 V, main continuous current 18.6 A against an illustrative installation-specific 25 A wire limit, and runtime 16.8 min against ≥15 min. These are mockup fixtures, not design approval or real component certification.
